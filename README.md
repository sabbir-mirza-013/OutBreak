
**Outbreak** is a 2D arcade-style brick‑breaker (Breakout) game. The player controls a paddle at the bottom of the screen and bounces a ball to destroy bricks arranged in levels. Bricks have colors that affect scoring and special effects.

**How to play**
Move the paddle left/right to position the ball.
Press Space to launch/fire the ball.
Match the ball's color with a brick's color to destroy that brick (same-color hits give full benefit).
Clear all bricks to progress to the next level.
You have limited lives; missing the ball reduces life.
Use pause and restart as needed.

**Controls**

A or Left Arrow — move paddle left
D or Right Arrow — move paddle right
Space — shoot/launch the ball
P — pause / resume
R — restart
Esc — quit

**Overview / Mechanics**
Paddle and Ball: paddle width and speed can change; the ball has direction and color and bounces off walls, ceiling and paddle.
Bricks: multiple colors arranged by level. Hitting bricks of the same color as the ball awards points and may trigger bonuses.
Color mechanics: the ball cycles or changes color after hits; matching colors yields higher score and can trigger level-up or power-ups.
Power-ups / Boosters: certain bricks provide boosters (examples: purple and lightGrey) that can increase score, widen paddle, or trigger instant level progression.
Levels and progression: levels are predefined wall designs; clearing current bricks advances to next level. When all levels complete the game ends.
Scoring & difficulty: score increments on brick destruction; paddle speed and other parameters may increase as score grows.
Lives & Game Over: missing the ball reduces lives; when lives reach zero the game ends.

**UI**
On-screen menu shows play/pause and restart/quit icons.
Score, lives and level progression are reflected through gameplay events and printed messages.
